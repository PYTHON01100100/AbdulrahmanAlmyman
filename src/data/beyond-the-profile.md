# Beyond the Profile: Italy, Philosophy, and My Journey in Cloud & AI

*The story behind my LinkedIn photographs, my curiosity about the world, and my passion for Cloud Engineering and Artificial Intelligence.*

Salam Alaikum! (^.^)/ shabab

## Introduction

Recently, I decided to update my LinkedIn profile with two photographs from my travels in Italy: a portrait of myself by the Mediterranean Sea, and Raphael's famous painting, *The School of Athens*, which I photographed during my visit to Vatican City.

![My LinkedIn profile with the two photographs from Italy: my portrait by the Mediterranean Sea as the profile picture, and The School of Athens as the cover](/beyond-the-profile/linkedin-profile.png)

At first glance, these might seem like ordinary profile photographs. However, both hold personal meaning for me.

They represent my curiosity about the world, my appreciation for history and philosophy, and my passion for technology.

I believe an engineer's identity is shaped not only by technical skills, but also by the experiences, questions and ideas that inspire them.

## 01 — The Italian Sea: A World Beyond Technology

My LinkedIn profile picture was captured in Italy, on the coastal road I walked between **Santa Margherita Ligure and Portofino**, with the Mediterranean behind me.

![Me smiling on the coastal road between Santa Margherita Ligure and Portofino in Italy, with the blue Mediterranean Sea, a boat and the green hills of the coast behind me](/beyond-the-profile/italy-portofino-road.jpg)

Traveling has always been one of my favorite ways to explore the world. I enjoy discovering new cities, experiencing different cultures, learning about history, and appreciating art and architecture.

Standing beside the Mediterranean Sea reminded me how much there is to discover beyond our everyday routines.

As an engineer, I spend much of my time working with Linux, cloud infrastructure, distributed systems and artificial intelligence. These are fields I genuinely enjoy, but I also value the experiences that take me away from the computer screen.

I chose this photograph because it represents the human side of my journey.

Behind every engineer is a person with interests, dreams, and a unique perspective on the world.

And sometimes, our most meaningful ideas begin far away from our desks.

## 02 — The School of Athens: Knowledge, Reason, and Artificial Intelligence

During my visit to Vatican City, I had the opportunity to see and photograph one of the most remarkable works of Renaissance art: *The School of Athens*, painted by Raphael.

![My photograph of Raphael's fresco The School of Athens in Vatican City, with Plato and Aristotle at the center surrounded by philosophers and mathematicians](/beyond-the-profile/school-of-athens-vatican.jpg)

The painting brings together some of history's greatest philosophers, mathematicians and thinkers.

At its center stand Plato and Aristotle, representing different approaches to understanding reality, knowledge, and the world around us.

What fascinates me most about this painting is not only its artistic beauty, but also what it represents: humanity's continuous pursuit of knowledge.

Centuries later, we continue exploring many of the same fundamental questions, now through mathematics, computer science and artificial intelligence.

- What does it mean to understand something?
- How do humans acquire knowledge?
- Can machines reason, learn, and solve problems?
- What are the responsibilities that come with building increasingly capable intelligent systems?

These questions are part of what makes artificial intelligence such a fascinating field to me.

Of course, the painting was created long before computers or modern AI existed. But I see a philosophical connection between the intellectual curiosity of its thinkers and the scientific questions we continue to explore today.

That is why I selected my photograph of *The School of Athens* as my LinkedIn cover.

It reminds me that technology is part of a much larger human story of discovery.

## 03 — Cloud Engineering: My Technical Foundation

While I am deeply interested in artificial intelligence, my strongest technical foundation is Cloud Engineering.

My engineering journey has involved cloud platforms such as Alibaba Cloud, AWS and Google Cloud, along with technologies including Linux, Kubernetes, Terraform, networking, containers and infrastructure automation.

I enjoy understanding how systems operate beneath the surface.

- How are distributed applications designed?
- How do we build reliable and scalable infrastructure?
- How can networking, storage, compute resources and automation work together efficiently?

These are the engineering challenges I enjoy solving.

Through my professional experience at SCCC by stc, I have continued expanding my knowledge of cloud technologies while exploring their applications in machine learning and generative AI.

I believe that cloud infrastructure plays an essential role in making modern AI possible.

Large language models require more than sophisticated algorithms. They depend on computing infrastructure, GPUs, networking, storage, orchestration, deployment pipelines and reliable operational practices.

This is where my interests in Cloud Engineering and Artificial Intelligence naturally come together.

**Cloud Engineering is my foundation. AI Infrastructure is one of the areas where I continue to expand my expertise.**

Rather than seeing them as separate disciplines, I see an opportunity to combine both to build better systems.

## 04 — Building, Learning, and Open Source

I believe that one of the best ways to grow as an engineer is to build things.

Beyond my professional responsibilities, I enjoy experimenting with technologies, developing tools, contributing to open-source projects, and sharing what I learn with the wider engineering community.

Whether I am exploring Linux, building cloud infrastructure tools, experimenting with Kubernetes, or deploying language models, I try to approach each project as an opportunity to learn something new.

Not every experiment succeeds, and not every solution works on the first attempt.

But every challenge provides an opportunity to improve.

For me, engineering is not simply about knowing how to use a technology.

It is about understanding why something works, identifying its limitations, and finding better ways to build it.

## 05 — NAMAA: Contributing to the Future of Arabic AI

Another meaningful part of my journey is my involvement with NAMAA, a community focused on advancing Arabic Natural Language Processing and Artificial Intelligence.

As a contributor to the community, I value the opportunity to collaborate with people who share an interest in developing Arabic AI technologies and strengthening the Arabic open-source ecosystem.

Arabic is a rich language with an extraordinary cultural and intellectual history.

I believe it is important for Arabic to have a strong presence in modern AI research, datasets, language models and technological development.

Communities like NAMAA demonstrate the value of collaboration, knowledge sharing and collective progress.

Being part of that effort is something I genuinely appreciate.

## 06 — Looking Ahead: Engineering, Curiosity, and Collaboration

My journey in technology continues to be driven by three things: curiosity, engineering, and continuous learning.

I want to keep strengthening my understanding of cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, platform engineering, and the infrastructure powering modern artificial intelligence.

I also value collaborating with engineers from different backgrounds, exchanging ideas, and contributing to meaningful technical projects and open-source communities.

There is always another system to understand, another challenge to solve, and another idea worth exploring.

That is what makes engineering exciting to me.

## Final Thoughts

Looking at my LinkedIn profile, you will see two photographs that represent different sides of my personality.

The Mediterranean Sea reminds me to explore the world beyond technology.

*The School of Athens* reminds me to question, reason, and pursue knowledge.

And my work in Cloud Engineering and Artificial Intelligence gives me the opportunity to turn curiosity into practical solutions.

**I believe the best engineers never stop being students of the world.**

Keep exploring. Keep questioning. Keep learning. Keep building.
